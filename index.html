Baking instructions
  1. Place the frozen pie on a heavy baking tray. Don’t thaw it first.
  2. Egg-wash the top if you’d like a glazed crust.
  3. Bake at 400°F: small pies 35–45 minutes, large pies 45–55 minutes, until golden brown and bubbly.
  4. Let it rest 10–15 minutes to set up. Enjoy!

Our story

A family pie shop since 1983

The Nadeau family opened That Pie Place on Kerr Street in Oakville in 1983. In 1993 the family switched to a wholesale model to serve more people, and to keep money going into ingredients instead of overhead.

Beef comes from rump roast we hand-trim ourselves. Chicken pies use white breast meat cooked in our own stock. Turkeys are roasted whole and stripped by hand, and the carcass becomes the stock for the filling.

Our Tourtière is a family recipe, and we still make it the same way we did in 1983. We cook the way you would at home, with simple spicing and very little salt. You’re welcome to come by and visit.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Should I thaw a pie before cooking?

No. All unbaked products are designed to be cooked from frozen. Thawing does little to cut the cooking time. The water in the gravy thaws first and can leave the gravy, so the pie may turn out runny and the crust soggy.

Why didn’t the bottom crust cook?

The aluminum plates conduct heat so fast that on their own they can’t cook the bottom crust. Always put the frozen pie on a baking tray or cookie sheet. The heavier the better.

Do you have custom sizes?

Yes. We generally use a 9×13 foil container that holds 8–12 portions, and pies made this way have a top crust only. You can also drop off your own dishes and we’ll fill them for special occasions. We just need a few days, so order ahead.

Do you make pies with just a top crust?

Yes. Every pie is filled by hand, so we can make any pie in any size with just a top crust. There’s no minimum order; just give us a few days.

Are you nut free?

No. We make tarts with pecans. We have good production policies to reduce the risk of cross-contamination, but we can’t guarantee products are nut free.

Which allergens are on site?
  • Fish: anchovies in the Worcestershire sauce
  • Wheat: all products (except Shepherd’s Pie)
  • Egg: all products
  • Milk: quiche, tarts, and products with cheese and butter
  • Tree nuts: pecans in the butter tarts
Why the boxed packaging?

Plastic wrap wasn’t standing up to handling, and damaged pies had to be replaced or thrown out. The box protects the pie much better for food safety (it was recommended to us by provincial food inspectors), gives us room for all the labelling we’re required to include, and looks better on our retailers’ shelves.
